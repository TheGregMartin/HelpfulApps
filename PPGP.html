EGO calculator and Adjust PPGP helper

Raising C from 39% to 40% and lowering D from 11% to 10% is a valid PPGP adjustment. It requires double-marking 30 boxes from the Progress Points grid on your polity screen (15 for the C column + 15 for the D column). Tap here to try other combinations, or tap “Reset entire PPGP table”.

Adjusting your PPGP involves adding 1 to a column that’s 25% or higher, while subtracting 1 from a column that’s 25% or lower. To examine your options, tap the upper or lower half of a column to raise or lower a gene type.
